# What did a Roman legionary at Alesia (52 BC) wear and carry?

Reading answered: the kit of an ordinary legionary in Caesar's army in Gaul, 52 BC, as far as open primary texts and dated collection records show.

He carried a large curved shield with a leather cover, a Spanish sword (gladius hispaniensis) on the right thigh and two pila, and wore a crested bronze helmet [s1][s2]. Polybius, about a century earlier, adds greaves and a small square bronze heart-protector, with a mail coat for wealthier men [s1]; Caesar's own account of 57 BC confirms helmets, shield covers and badges [s2]. A bronze Montefortino-type helmet (Met 2015.758.1) and a 3rd-1st century BC gladius from Hispania are the nearest dated objects in open collections [s3][s4]. The siege itself ran from about July to September 52 BC [s5].
